About Kylas Associates

A law firm founded by Dr. K. P. Kylasanatha Pillay, Senior Advocate, with offices in Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram and Noida.

Our firm

Legal practice across courts, tribunals and advisory matters

Kylas Associates was founded by Dr. K. P. Kylasanatha Pillay, a designated Senior Advocate practising before the Supreme Court of India. Over the years, the firm has grown into a team of ten advocates supported by dedicated office staff, with offices in Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram and Noida.

The firm handles a wide range of civil and criminal matters, writ petitions and public interest litigation, with service and education law at the centre of its practice. Its work extends from trial courts in Ernakulam and Thiruvananthapuram districts to the High Court of Kerala, the High Court of Delhi, the High Court of Karnataka, tribunals and the Supreme Court of India, with civil, criminal and writ matters also handled in Bengaluru.

Alongside litigation, the firm undertakes the registration of property documents, societies and trusts, the drafting of deeds and wills, and advisory work in co-operative, mining and geology, land conversion and intellectual property matters.

Three members of the firm are mediators trained by the Mediation and Conciliation Project Committee (MCPC) of the Supreme Court of India, and the firm encourages the amicable settlement of disputes wherever it is in the client's interest.
