Experienced counsel before courts and tribunals

Kylas Associates provides litigation services in Administrative, Constitutional, Civil, Criminal and Family Law before the High Court, the Supreme Court of India, Tribunals and various other courts and forums.

The firm also offers legal consultation, due diligence, drafting and review of contracts, legal notices, statutory appeals, revisions, representations and title verification.

How a matter proceeds

What to expect from your first call to the final hearing.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Tell us the facts. We listen, ask for the documents we need and explain your options.

  2. 2

    Review

    We study the papers, the applicable law, and the questions of forum and limitation.

  3. 3

    Strategy

    You get a clear plan: what to file, where to file it, and what to expect.

  4. 4

    Drafting and filing

    Petitions, pleadings, notices and representations are prepared with you and filed.

  5. 5

    Appearance and follow-up

    We appear before the court or tribunal and keep you informed at every stage.

Frequently asked questions

What areas of law does the firm handle?

The firm advises and represents clients in administrative and service law, constitutional, civil, criminal, family, environmental and education matters before courts and tribunals. See Areas of practice for details.

How can I arrange a consultation?

You may contact the firm by telephone, email or through the enquiry form below. Please share only a brief description of the matter until the firm confirms how documents should be shared.

Can consultations be conducted online?

Online consultations may be available where an in-person meeting is unnecessary. The suitable mode depends on the documents and nature of the matter.

Does contacting the firm create a lawyer-client relationship?

No. Sending an enquiry or documents does not by itself create a lawyer-client relationship. It arises only after the firm accepts the engagement and the terms are confirmed.

How are professional fees determined?

Fees depend on the nature, complexity, urgency and expected scope of the work, and are discussed before an assignment is accepted.

Can the firm guarantee the outcome of my case?

No lawyer can guarantee the result of a legal proceeding. The outcome depends on the facts, evidence, applicable law and the decision of the competent court or tribunal.
