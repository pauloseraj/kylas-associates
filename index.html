About the firm

A considered approach to legal work

Kylas Associates was founded by Dr. K. P. Kylasanatha Pillay, Senior Advocate, and has grown into a team of ten advocates supported by dedicated office staff.

The firm handles civil, criminal, constitutional, service, education, family and regulatory matters, together with registration, conveyancing and mediation. Its work extends across trial courts, High Courts, the Supreme Court of India and tribunals.

Alongside litigation, the firm undertakes property-document registration, drafting of deeds and wills, registration of societies and trusts, and advisory work in selected regulatory fields.
