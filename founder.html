Dr. K. P. Kylasanatha Pillay

Founder, Kylas Associates

Dr. K. P. Kylasanatha Pillay is a Senior Advocate of the Supreme Court of India and the founder of Kylas Associates. Enrolled as an advocate in February 1987, he practised before the High Court of Kerala and other courts until 2005, when he moved his practice to the Supreme Court of India. He was designated a Senior Advocate in 2009.

He holds an LL.M. and a Ph.D. in law, and Master's degrees in English and History.

dr.kppillay@gmail.com · +91 98710 17940

Practice and appointments

Professional experience

His principal areas of practice are constitutional law, administrative law, corporate law, jurisprudence, and arbitration and mediation. He is on the panel of Senior Advocates representing the Central Government before the Supreme Court of India, and is a Senior Panel Member of the Supreme Court Legal Services Committee.

He is a mediator accredited by the Mediation and Conciliation Project Committee (MCPC) of the Supreme Court of India, and is empanelled as a mediator at the Mediation Centre of the Supreme Court of India.

He served as a Member and Vice-Chairman of the Bar Council of Kerala from 1997 to 2005, and as an Independent Director of NTPC Limited from 2018 to 2021. He is a member of the Supreme Court Bar Association and the Kerala High Court Advocates' Association.

Academic engagement

Teaching, research and public programmes

Alongside his practice, Dr. K. P. Kylasanatha Pillay has taught law for over three decades. He taught undergraduate students as part-time faculty from 1986 to 1998, served as a Resource Person at the National Judicial Academy, Bhopal (2006–2007), and has been Visiting Faculty at the Indian Law Institute.

As an approved research guide at the National University of Advanced Legal Studies (NUALS) from 2012 to 2021, he guided two scholars, both Associate Professors at Government Law College, Thrissur, to their doctorates. He is presently Senior Visiting Professor for the LL.M. programme at the Kerala Law Academy.

He is the mentor of CG3 (Connecting Governed, Governing, Governance), a hub for legal research and public programmes, through which he has guided legal awareness programmes for students, workshops for elected representatives of local bodies in Kerala, mediation training for lawyers and coaching for UGC NET and JRF aspirants.
