Privacy Policy

How information submitted through this website is described as being used.

Kylas Associates respects the privacy of visitors to this website. Personal information submitted through the enquiry form, such as your name, email address, phone number and message, is used only to respond to your enquiry and is not sold, rented or shared with third parties except where required by law.

This version of the website uses browser local storage to remember your acceptance of the entry disclaimer. It does not include analytics or advertising trackers. The office locations page embeds Google Maps; when that page is opened, Google may receive technical information through the embedded map, subject to Google’s own terms and privacy policy. Enquiry messages are not stored by this static website. Any information sent by email is handled through the visitor’s email provider and the firm’s email accounts.

For any questions about this policy, or to request correction or deletion of your information, please write to kylasernakulam@gmail.com or kylasachuth@gmail.com.

The current static enquiry form opens a draft email in the visitor’s email application. It does not send or store form submissions on this website’s hosting server. The visitor must review and send the email.
